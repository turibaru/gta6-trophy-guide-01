Roadmap to the Platinum

“Vice Got a Price!” pops when the other 54 are done. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 difficulty, about 200 hours, in a single playthrough; Push Square estimates up to 300+ hours. All 55 are offline — no online trophies. Leave cheats off: cheats disable trophies and saving (GTABase / TechWiser, via eGamers).

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story. Just enjoy it. The 12 story trophies and the final-mission Gold pop on their own, and free roam continues after the story (Push Square). Do one thing early: meditate at the first meditation spot in Chapter 1 — see the missable warning below.
  2. Phase 2 — Strangers & side characters. Finish the stranger mission strands (8 needed for the Silver; 14 exist, so skip the ones you dislike) and the final mission of each named character — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone.
  3. Phase 3 — Activities. Shooting ranges (gold in all 42), drone races (win at all 14 spots), races, fishing, hunting, arcade, golf, pool, basketball — and 250 stunts.
  4. Phase 4 — Collecting & exploring. Cards (3 of 18 sets), 100 WAiNK tags, 100 vehicles, all of Leonida.
  5. Phase 5 — Crime & money. Robberies, vaults, armored trucks, laundering $250,000 in Marked Bills, buying all 8 garages.
  6. Phase 6 — Cleanup & 100%. The in-game completion checklist opens after the story (Push Square). Finish the remaining challenges (50 for the Bronze, 70 toward 100%) and mop up anything left.

⚠ Only one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot (18). PowerPyx and GTABase flag it because the first meditation spot is only usable early in the story, in Chapter 1. Meditate there as soon as it appears and keep a manual save from before the end of Chapter 1.
