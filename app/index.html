Roadmap to the platinum

Six phases, in the order to play them. Counts update live from your checklist. Built from the official trophy descriptions: story chapters first, then strangers, collectibles, activities, and the crime & economy cleanup — the platinum pops when the 54th trophy does.

Missable trophies

Only one of the 55 trophies can be missed. Everything else can be earned after the story ends, in free roam or in the cleanup phase.

How the other trophies stay safe

  • Story trophies (Chapters 1–4, the named missions, the finale) unlock by playing the story; none of them is missable — missions can be replayed.
  • Stranger strands stay open after the story, so Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone can be finished in the cleanup phase.
  • Collectibles (cards, WAiNK spots), stunts, challenges, races and the shooting range are all still available in free roam.
  • Keep a save from before major story choices: two hidden trophies (Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess) read like choice trophies, but their exact triggers are unconfirmed — see their cards.

What “Method unconfirmed” means

9 trophies carry the label Method unconfirmed: their guides say only what the official description itself suggests, because no checked method has been published yet. We would rather mark a gap honestly than invent a walkthrough. Those 9 are: Challenging Tank (#38), In The Beginning… (#44), Capital Offense (#45), Let’s Go Bowling (#46), Personal Best (#47), Actions Speak Louder (#48), Bad Luck, I Guess (#49), The Truth Is Out There (#50) and Ten Pin (#51).
